01 Overview

Training built the hyperscale era. Inference is building the edge era.

EdgeFlex targets 25–75 MW metro-edge sites with power in hand — brownfields, powered shells, and land adjacent to live substations. Modular tranches are added against demand, not years ahead of it.

The founding team brings $40B+ of transaction experience across digital infrastructure and 22 executed transactions spanning data centers, powered land, equity, and debt. Deep expertise in data center development, principal investing, power trading, and digital infrastructure capital markets informs site selection, underwriting, procurement, and entitlements. The platform controls a 500+ MW active development pipeline across every major U.S. power market.

Every prompt, agent step, API call, and token served runs a trained model. Usage turns directly into recurring compute demand — and that demand compounds with adoption. Training was an episodic capital event; inference is the model in use, every day, everywhere.

Agentic AI, real-time voice, autonomy, and industrial automation demand sub-10ms responses that centralized hyperscale campuses cannot deliver. Serving tokens near users cuts backhaul and networking cost, while data-residency mandates in 40+ countries push workloads into regional and metro-edge facilities.

The workload has shifted. Latency is the product.

03 Site Selection

Powered, entitled, and metro-proximate — or it fails the screen.

The Flagship

Metro-Edge Acquisition

Powered, entitled, metro-proximate sites acquired at brownfield basis — with interconnection signed before closing.

  • 25–75 MW IT per site, versus 300–1,000+ MW hyperscale campuses
  • 6–18 months to revenue on existing power, shells, and permits
  • Modular 5–20 MW tranches added against demand
Existing Interconnects

The acquisition screen requires an interconnection agreement in hand or signed before closing. Sub-75 MW phases avoid large-load study batching.

Low Latency

Metro-edge locations sit inside or adjacent to major population and enterprise centers, with carrier diversity and international-airport access.

Existing Infrastructure

Adjacent or on-site substations provide a live electrical meter for diligence pricing and expedite access to power; brownfields retain infrastructure in place.

Entitlement Clarity

Data center by-right zoning, existing industrial permits, or municipal confirmation precedes commitment. Rezoning campaigns fail the screen.
